Last year, my team shipped a Swift rewrite of Apple's TrueType hinting interpreter. The new interpreter is strictly memory safe, relentlessly tested, and faster than the code it replaced.

This year, it is my pleasure to ship it for a second time as an [open source package](https://github.com/apple/truetype-hinting-interpreter-example), and to share our experience on the [Swift blog](https://www.swift.org/blog/migrating-truetype-hinting-to-swift/).
