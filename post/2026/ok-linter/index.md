For a moment it became popular to call agents "clankers", a practice that spiked in response to the terminally enlightened having a crisis about the term being a slur.

I only participated in this ironically, for fear of retribution. At the time this was happening,[^coincident] I was often finding myself in situations where I would be making changes in a project alongside an agent, and it would occasionally run into my edits and rationalize the experience with "the linter must have made some changes while I was working". Given the trends of the industry at the time,[^grief] the concept of being called a linter was *mortifying*.[^humour]

[^as-now]: And still today, among some slice of people.
[^coincident]: Before the agents (or I) had learned to use worktrees properly, and coincident with "[okay boomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OK_boomer)".
[^grief]: And how nearly all of us were solidly experiencing some stage of grief about it.
[^humour]: Meanwhile, the agents seem to [have a good sense of humour](clanker-on-duty.png "A Screenshot of Claude Code, in which I ask it to debug a problem with this very post, it identifies a dumb syntax error, I reply "lol who's the linter now <3", and the response comes back "Happy to be the clanker on duty."").
