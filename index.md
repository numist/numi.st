Hi! I'm [Scott Perry](/about), and this is where I [write things](/blog) that I might want to refer to later[^social], on topics that range from [economics](/post/2022/what-is-mmt/), [misadventure](/post/2022/facepalm/), and whatever's <a target="_self" href="{{ site.published.first.link | default: site.published.first.url | relative_url }}">on the mind lately</a>. This site is made to be explored[^garden]; by category, you'll find:

* [online tools](tool) I've built to make my life easier
* [ideas](ideas){: data-no-preview="" } I couldn't get out of my head
* [stories](stories){: data-no-preview="" } of things that have happened to me (or that I did to myself)
* [code](code){: data-no-preview="" } snippets, solutions to small problems I've had and ideas that have struck me
* [projects](projects){: data-no-preview="" } throughout their lifecycle
* [stuff I use](iusethis) that makes me happy (or not) in the hopes that it'll make you happy (or not?) too
